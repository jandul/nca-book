Necessary Condition Analysis (NCA)

C Publications

This appendix classifies the 583 English-language scholarly articles published or in press in journals listed in Clarivate’s Journal Citation Reports. Articles with problematic applications of NCA (see Dul, 2022) and articles in journals that are considered ‘predatory journals’ (https://predatoryjournals.org/) are excluded from the list. Being listed does not automatically imply that NCA is properly described and applied (see Section 10.3 for a checklist with guidelines and recommendations).

Figure C.1 shows the growth of the cumulative number of scholarly articles that apply NCA. About two-thirds of the articles appear in the highest journal rank category by impact factor (Q1) with Q1 = first quartile, Q2 = second quartile, etc.; Q? indicates that no quality rank is available yet.

Figure C.1: Cumulative number of articles that apply NCA published in Web of Science (Clarivate) listed journals. (Situation of 31 December 2025).
